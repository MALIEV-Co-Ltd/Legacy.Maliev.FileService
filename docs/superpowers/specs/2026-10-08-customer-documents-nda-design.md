# Customer documents and NDA protection — draft specification
Date: 2026-10-08. Tracker: Intranet#277. Status: complete self-reviewed design; advance execution scope read back from owner comment6053052177 after initial automatic-review rejection. No product changes yet.

## Intent and placement
Employees and explicitly authorized members manage customer documents and externally executed NDAs. The existing order social-publication choice is preserved. Agreement expiry never releases confidential work.
Use a distinct CustomerDocuments business module in Legacy.Maliev.FileService, additive to existing storage responsibilities. Keep legacy Upload mapping unchanged, CustomerService profile-only, Auth identity-only and DocumentService renderer-only. No new service, repository, infrastructure or live grants. Alternatives are a standalone registry (extra deployment/coordination) and a CustomerService extension (violates current profile boundary); the module is the smallest coherent first release.
Use existing .NET10/EF Core/PostgreSQL, FileService quarantine/full-file scanner/generation adapters, resource permissions and same-origin BFFs. Feature flags default disabled until a separate runtime activation decision.

## Durable records and minimal evidence contract
New tables: CustomerDocument (Guid Id, CustomerId, Kind, Title, Visibility, ArchivedAtUtc, Revision/xmin); CustomerDocumentVersion (Guid Id, DocumentId, positive VersionNumber, private coordinates/exact generation, SHA256, detected type/size, clean scan operation/evidence, ActorSubject, CreatedAtUtc); DocumentAssociation (DocumentId, Kind, positive ResourceId, confirmed CustomerId); DocumentAudit (append-only actor/time/action/reason/version/revision); NdaRecord (DocumentId and exact agreement VersionId, explicit parties, start/effective/expiry/renewal dates, SurvivalEndsAtUtc or Indefinite/Unknown, verification state/actor/time, ResponsibleEmployeeSubject, Revision); NdaCoverage (NdaId, canonical CustomerId, ResourceKind/Id or customer-wide); InternalNdaReminder (agreement/version/renewal revision/lead-days, responsible employee, due date, state, unique deduplication key).
Document kinds: Nda, Corporate, BillingInstruction, Shipment, Release, Acceptance, Evidence. Visibility is Internal or Customer; customer-created documents cannot become Internal and cannot change visibility after upload. Employee publication of a version as member-visible requires explicit write authority and audit.
Billing's immutable receipt contains Guid DocumentId/VersionId, int CustomerId, Kind, ContentSha256, explicit int? QuotationId and int[] OrderIds, VerificationStatus, VerifiedBySubject, VerifiedAtUtc and positive long Revision. PascalCase JSON Kind and VerificationStatus are named strings (Verified exactly "Verified"), never ordinals. Producer hashes are lowercase64hex; consumers compare case-insensitively. Verified receipt actor is nonblank and time is UTC. It contains no storage paths, URL, unrelated versions, internal notes or agreement body. Accounting retains its own immutable issue snapshots and does not own registry persistence. Do not conflate commercial request, tax invoice and receipt.
Resource kinds initially Customer, Order, Quotation, Invoice, Replacement, Shipment. IDs are scalar references confirmed by the owning service. Unimplemented Project/work identities are rejected, not trusted because the UI supplies a number. Replacement relationships remain Order-owned and inherit explicit original coverage. CompanyId/name/email domain never conveys membership or legal coverage.
Verification is a separate append-only DocumentVerificationEvidence record keyed by VersionId and positive Revision. Upload creates PendingVerification; staff verification appends a new revision rather than rewriting immutable version bytes or captured associations. Receipt returns the latest authoritative verification revision for the selected exact version; Accounting snapshots that revision and retains issued history. An old snapshot is not an override permitting new issuance after current authority changes.

## Authority and lifecycle
Every endpoint authenticates through normal JWT middleware, evaluates an explicit customer resource permission and checks identity kind/current session according to existing Auth conventions. Member CustomerId is server-established from trusted identity; a caller-supplied ID cannot change tenant. Employee authority is resource-scoped, never inferred from general file permission. Define constants for documents.read, documents.write, documents.verify, documents.archive and protection.evaluate; do not grant them to live identities.
Verifier and responsible staff are explicit active employee subjects. Member uploads remain PendingVerification after clean scanning. Staff verification records the exact signed version, parties, coverage, effective/start date, expiry and separate survival data; no e-signing, no automatic execution, no customer uploader self-verification. Verification/update uses concurrency and append-only audit. Renewal creates a new exact agreement version/record and supersedes the agreement lifecycle without deleting old obligations.
Agreement calendar status (future/active/expired/superseded) and confidentiality obligation (protected/review required/released by separately authorized later workflow) are separate. V1 never exposes an obligation-release/override endpoint. Unknown or indefinite survival remains protected. Explicit finite survival ending still requires reviewed release; the calendar cannot publish files.

## API outline (PascalCase JSON, additive routes)
GET /customers/{customerId:int}/documents — bounded authorized summaries; Internal metadata omitted for members.
POST /customers/{customerId:int}/documents — multipart files plus validated category/title/visibility, idempotency key; clean upload creates v1, returns metadata only.
POST /customers/{customerId:int}/documents/{documentId:guid}/versions — immutable replacement upload; concurrent version increments serialized, pending NDA verification not silently inherited.
GET /customers/{customerId:int}/documents/{documentId:guid}/versions/{versionId:guid}/receipt — authorized exact immutable evidence receipt.
GET /customers/{customerId:int}/documents/{documentId:guid}/versions/{versionId:guid}/download — authenticated attachment proxy; no bearer URL in browser.
POST /customers/{customerId:int}/documents/{documentId:guid}/nda/verification — employee-only exact-version verification and explicit coverage, expected revision.
POST /customers/{customerId:int}/documents/{documentId:guid}/archive — permission/reason/concurrency; no physical original deletion.
GET /customers/{customerId:int}/protection?kind=Order&id=... — actor/resource-authorized coverage indicators for UI.
POST /customers/{customerId:int}/protection/evaluate — service/actor authorization plus action/resource/version; no client-selected override.
GET /staff/nda-reminders — employee-only responsible-staff worklist, bounded date/status filters and scoped permissions; customer notices absent.
Use typed results: 400 malformed, 401 unauthenticated, 403 authority denied, 404 missing/invisible resource, 409 concurrency/idempotency conflict, 413 oversized, 415 unsupported type, 422 malware/invalid agreement, 503 authoritative dependency or scanner unavailable. Do not return internal provider details.

## Upload, download and bypass protection
Feature uploads accept one bounded20MiB file per operation, PDF/PNG/JPEG only initially; detect content versus extension/MIME, reject zero/truncated/mismatched files, archive containers and unsafe names. Documents are attachment-only, never rendered as active HTML. Limits are feature-specific; do not change legacy limits.
Reserve durable version identity/idempotency fingerprint before storage. Quarantine is private under a registry-reserved prefix. Full-file scan must certify exact immutable bytes; promote through journaled generation move only after clean scan, commit exact version evidence and audit atomically. Unavailable/unknown/error/infected is never available or verifiable. Lost-response paths remain pending/unknown and reconcile exact generation, never delete by name or claim not uploaded.
Do not call legacy UploadAsync in a way that creates and returns a bearer URL before registry authority. Add a protected upload adapter using scanner/storage/journal boundaries directly or an explicitly metadata-only clean-upload mode proven not to sign.
Proxy download reevaluates tenant/classification/protection/current version and confirms clean journal evidence, live generation, size and hash. Reuse private storage adapter with exact-generation read. Buffer bounded content before returning attachment so evidence failure produces no partial content. Cache-Control:no-store; Content-Disposition safe attachment; nosniff. Audit successful authorization/receipt transfer without logging URL/body.
Reserve protected registry object prefix at every legacy generic read/move/delete/sign route; a direct bucket/object request must not bypass registry policy or mutate protected originals. Existing legacy routes retain other behavior. Fail closed for evidence unavailable.

## Enforcement and UI
Read confidentiality is scoped access, not a blanket ban on legitimate customer/staff work. ExternalShare, PublishSocial and PublicExport deny any protected or review-required coverage; explicit consent is additionally required for public work even without NDA. AllowSocialMedia values/checkbox remain unchanged. V1 does not issue public share links or disclosure exceptions.
Intranet customer view adds Documents and NDA sections, version history, upload progress, archive/verify with reason, responsible staff and expiry/survival badges; order/work views show authoritative coverage warnings independently of consent. BFF uses existing employee session/authority and CSRF, forwards bounded multipart server-side, never exposes service tokens.
Web adds /Member/Documents and member navigation; derives CustomerId from authenticated server session, lists/uploads/downloads only member-visible authorized documents and clean versions; uploaded NDA says Pending staff verification. Thai/English labels, keyboard forms and accessible progress/denied/conflict/error states.
Actual output producers must call the policy boundary before access/download/export/share/publication. Inventory existing public/social/image/export endpoints in implementation; if a producer cannot establish authority it refuses the protected operation. Do not invent a manufacturing JobService: original JobService is recruitment. Order associations cover known manufacturing work until an authoritative project/job contract is approved.

## Retention and internal reminders
V1 archive-only; originals, signed versions, audit and obligations retained indefinitely by default. Legal hold blocks archive/destructive future actions. Future deletion policy requires separate category/legal approval.
Internal reminders are durable in-app responsible-staff tasks with default lead days30,14,7,1,0, configurable explicit timezone (default Asia/Bangkok) and daily evaluation. TimeProvider controls clock. Atomic unique agreement/version/renewal-revision/lead-day key prevents duplicate jobs; renewal cancels stale future tasks without erasing audit; disabled scheduler produces none. Recipient is verified responsible employee only, no customer address resolution. No external transport or Notification caller-policy broadening in v1; later internal email requires its own approved adapter. Surface missed/uncertain processing and escalation to staff worklist.
No live sends occur in implementation/tests.
